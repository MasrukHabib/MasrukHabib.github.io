UR3 CobotOps – Enhancing Cobot Efficiency with ML

Implemented various ML techniques to optimize the UR3 collaborative robot, focusing on predictive maintenance and anomaly detection. Used ARIMA for time series forecasting, LSTM for sequential pattern recognition, K-Means for operational state clustering, and classification models for fault prediction. Trained on a UCI dataset, with plans to integrate real-time sensor data and explore reinforcement learning for further optimization.

About Me

I'm a passionate AI Engineer, Data Analyst and research enthusiast with a B.Tech in Computer Engineering, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

Currently, I work as a Research & Development Engineer at GoScurry.ai, contributing to AI-driven solutions involving LLMs, RAG pipelines and intelligent automation. Previously, as a Data Analyst at Jatri, I analysed sales, marketing, finance and supply-chain data to support decision-making — building dashboards, automating reports and tracking KPIs. I've also shipped backend features for CRM Squirrel using Python-based technologies.

My research spans ASD prediction, Alzheimer's detection, psychological well-being assessment and AI-driven drug-interaction analysis — published at IEEE international conferences. As a Student Research Mentor at Marwadi University's Research Activities Club, I guided three batches of students through research methodology and publication, leading 37 training sessions along the way.

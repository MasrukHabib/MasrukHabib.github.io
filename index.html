I'm an AI Engineer and Entrepreneur who builds technology that solves real problems. With a B.Tech in Computer Engineering, I work across Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Large Language Models and Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and I care just as much about turning that work into products people rely on.

I'm the Co-Founder of Sohoj Dokan, an e-commerce and store-builder platform helping small businesses and Facebook-based sellers in Bangladesh take their shops online. I look after the company's day-to-day operations and contribute to parts of the platform's development.

Alongside that, I work as a Research & Development Engineer at GoScurry.ai, building AI-driven products with LLMs, RAG pipelines and intelligent automation. Before this, as a Data Analyst at Jatri, I turned sales, marketing, finance and supply-chain data into dashboards, automated reports and KPIs, and I shipped backend features for CRM Squirrel in Python.

My research spans ASD prediction, Alzheimer's detection, psychological well-being assessment and AI-driven drug-interaction analysis, published at IEEE international conferences. As a Student Research Mentor at Marwadi University, I guided three batches of students through research and publication across 37 training sessions.
